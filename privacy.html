Privacy Policy

UoN GPA · CGPA Calculator — Last updated: October 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the UoN GPA · CGPA Calculator app ("the app") handles information. The app is developed and published by mAs87. It is an unofficial, independent tool built to help University of Narowal students calculate their GPA/CGPA, and is not an official University publication.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to our own servers. All data you enter into the calculator — subjects, credit hours, grades, marks, and calculated results — is saved only on your own device, using your device's local storage. We cannot see, access, or retrieve this data. The app also keeps a few small settings on your device (for example, whether you have purchased "Remove Ads").

The app shows advertisements provided by a third-party ad network. That network may collect certain information from your device while the app is running, as described in the "Advertising" section below. We do not receive or control this data.

The app's screens use fonts that are loaded from Google Fonts. When your device downloads these fonts, Google receives standard connection information such as your IP address, in line with Google's Privacy Policy. We do not use any analytics or tracking tools of our own.

Advertising

The app displays advertisements using Start.io (formerly StartApp), a third-party advertising network. To show and measure ads, the Start.io software included in the app may collect and use information from your device, including:

  • your IP address, device properties, mobile carrier, and network connection details;
  • your Android advertising ID (a resettable identifier used for advertising);
  • your approximate or precise location, where location is available on your device.

When you first open the app, Start.io shows a privacy dialog asking whether you agree to the collection of "Advanced Data" (such as installed apps, location, Bluetooth paired devices, and device sensors) for the purpose of personalized advertising across the Start.io network and its data partners. If you tap Agree, this Advanced Data may be collected. If you tap Disagree, Advanced Data will not be collected.

This information is collected and processed by Start.io and its partners under their own privacy policies, not by us. You can read more here: Start.io Trust Center (terms and privacy).

In-App Purchase (Remove Ads)

The app offers an optional one-time purchase called "Remove Ads". Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive, see, or store your payment details. After a successful purchase, the app stores a simple "ads removed" setting on your device so that ads are no longer shown.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over (university students). It is not directed at children, including children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, please contact us using the details below.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or store your personal data on any server, there is no server-side personal data held by us to secure. Information processed for advertising purposes is handled directly by Start.io and its partners, subject to their own security and privacy practices.

Your Choices

  • Choose Disagree in the Start.io privacy dialog to decline the collection of Advanced Data.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or limit ad personalization, in your phone's system settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or similar, depending on your device).
  • Turn off location access for the app or device in your phone's settings.
  • Purchase "Remove Ads" inside the app to stop ads from being shown.
  • Uninstall the app at any time; data stored by the calculator on your device is removed with it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the advertising partner changes. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
